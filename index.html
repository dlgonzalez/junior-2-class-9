The Life Backpack

Junior 2 - Class 10

Level A2+ Time: 2.5h

🎯 Class Objectives

  • ✓ Review Simple Past (Specific Time).
  • ✓ Discover Present Perfect (Life Experiences).
  • ✓ Play grammar games with Past Participles (V3).
  • ✓ Read & comprehend "School of Rock".
📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Transition & Warm-up:
1. Have students sit in a circle or push desks back so they can stand up and move easily.
2. Explain: "I will put a sentence on the board. If it is true for you, stand up and switch seats quickly!"
3. The goal is high energy. This uses the Simple Past (which they know).
4. The Bridge to Part 2: After the game, say: *"Notice how every sentence had a specific time like 'yesterday' or 'last year'? But what if we want to talk about amazing things we did in our lives, and it doesn't matter WHEN we did them? Let's find out!"*
🪑

📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Explaining Grammar:
1. Introduce the "Life Backpack" concept. Explain that experiences are things we carry with us always.
2. Point out the difference between V2 (Past) and V3 (Past Participle).
3. Drill the formula: Subject + HAVE/HAS + V3. Have students repeat the examples out loud.
🎒

Part 2: The Life Backpack

Simple Past (V2) 📅

A specific time in the past. It is finished.

  • • I went to Paris in 2022.
  • • She ate sushi yesterday.

Present Perfect (V3) ✨

A life experience! Time doesn't matter.

  • • I have gone to Paris.
  • • She has eaten sushi.

The Magic Formula

Subject + HAVE / HAS + Verb (V3)

I, You, We, They = HAVE   |   He, She, It = HAS

📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Running the Games:
Game 1: Call on individual students. Ask them: "What is the V3 of [Verb]?" When they guess, click the card to reveal the answer.
Game 2: Read the sentence to the class. Ask them to shout out "Specific Time" or "Life Experience" and click the button to sort it.

📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Reading "School of Rock":
1. Pre-read: Go through the vocabulary on the board. Make sure they understand the words.
2. Read: Read the text in the book together (or listen to the audio track).
3. The Reading Race: Divide class into 3-4 teams. Click 'Next Question'. Teams must scan the book text fast. The first student to stand up gets to answer. If correct, their team gets 1 point!

📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - The Detective Game:
1. Hand out small pieces of paper. Each student writes down ONE secret life experience using "I have..." (e.g., "I have broken an arm"). They hand them to the teacher.
2. Mix the papers up. Hand one paper to each student. THIS IS THEIR NEW SECRET IDENTITY.
3. Display the target profile on the board. Students must walk around asking "Have you ever...?" to find the student holding the paper that matches the board.
🕵️‍♂️

Part 5: The Undercover Agent

WANTED: Undercover Agent

Walk around the room. Ask your classmates questions to find the person who matches this profile!

Ask them:

  • 1. "Have you ever met a famous person?"
  • 2. "Have you ever traveled on an airplane?"
  • 3. "Have you ever eaten strange food?"
📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Timeline & Gallery Walk:
1. Pass out blank paper and markers.
2. Students draw a timeline of their life and write 3 things they have done (experiences) using Present Perfect.
3. Important: Direct their attention to the 3 examples on the board so they know exactly what to do.
4. Gallery Walk: Put the timelines on the desks. Students walk around, read their friends' timelines, and write a positive comment or draw a star on their favorites.
