📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Transition & Warm-up:

  • Have students sit in a circle or push desks back so they can stand up and move easily.
  • Say: "I will put a sentence on the board. If it is true for you, stand up and switch seats quickly!"
  • Click the 'Next Prompt' button to cycle through 15 different prompts using Simple Past.
  • The Bridge to Part 2: After the game, say: "Notice how every sentence had a specific time like 'yesterday' or 'last year'? But what if we want to talk about amazing things we did in our lives, and it doesn't matter WHEN we did them? Let's find out!"

📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Explaining Grammar:

  • Introduce the "Life Backpack" concept. Explain that experiences are things we carry with us always, in our invisible backpacks.
  • Point out the difference between V2 (Past) and V3 (Past Participle).
  • Drill the formula: Subject + HAVE/HAS + V3. Have students repeat the examples out loud.
🎒

Part 2: The Life Backpack

Simple Past (V2) 📅

A specific time in the past. It is finished.

  • • I went to Paris in 2022.
  • • She ate sushi yesterday.

Present Perfect (V3) ✨

A life experience! Time doesn't matter.

  • • I have gone to Paris.
  • • She has eaten sushi.

The Magic Formula

Subject + HAVE / HAS + Verb (V3)

I, You, We, They ➡️ HAVE
He, She, It ➡️ HAS

📝 SUB TEACHER GUIDE - Timeline to Paragraph:

  • Step 1: Have students brainstorm 3-4 experiences in their notebooks using the categories on the board.
  • Step 2: Explain the trick: Introduce the experience with Present Perfect, but give the details using Simple Past! Read the "Teacher Secret" example on the board together.
  • Step 3: Tell them to copy the Paragraph Formula onto a clean piece of paper and fill in the blanks with their own experiences. Have them draw a small picture at the bottom.
  • Step 4 (Gallery Walk): Students leave their papers on their desks. Everyone walks around, reads the paragraphs, and leaves a sticky note with a positive English comment (e.g., "Wow!", "Me too!", "Cool!").
✍️

Part 6: From Timeline to Paragraph

Step 1: Brainstorm Experiences 🧠

Write 3 or 4 things you HAVE DONE in your life using the Present Perfect.

✈️Travel:
"I have traveled to the snow."
🎸Skills:
"I have learned to play piano."
🐙Food:
"I have eaten octopus."
🏅Events:
"I have won a medal."

Step 2: Add the Details (The Teacher Secret!) 🤫

Introduce it with the Present Perfect, but tell the story using the Simple Past!

"I have eaten octopus! I ate it last year at the beach. It was delicious!"

Step 3: The Paragraph Formula 📝

Copy this format onto a clean piece of paper and fill in the blanks with your experiences!


I have had some amazing experiences in my short life.
First, I have _____________________________ (Experience 1).
I ______________ (past detail) and it ______________ (past detail).
Also, I have _____________________________ (Experience 2).
I ______________ (past detail) with my ______________.
Finally, I have _____________________________ (Experience 3).
This happened in ______________ (year) and I felt ______________!
I am excited to see what I will do next!
